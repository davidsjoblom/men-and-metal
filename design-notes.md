# Design Notes

## Project Pitch

Men & Metal is a competitive 1v1 miniatures wargame set in a contemporary near-peer conflict featuring intense clashes over key terrain between company sized forces.
It is designed ground-up for tournament play with a focus on watertight rules and strives to reward combined-arms tactics without overly restrictive army building.
Men & Metal aims to be simple to learn, hard to break and rewarding to master.

## Design Pillars

1. Unbreakable rules; rules should be watertight and at no point should players have to argue over rules interpretation.
2. Combined arms; the system should naturally reward different types of units working together and spamming a single unit entry should never be the most efficient strategy.
3. Intuitive mechanics; rules should have direct real-world analogs and interactions should never feel weird or gamey.
4. No disposable units; taking losses should matter, and using units in sacrificial ways should be rare and desperate not standard practice.
5. Terrain matters; terrain will be plentiful on the battlefield and central to the battle. Utilizing terrain properly is essential. 
6. Less is More; the least amount of complexity should be used and a strong focus on time-efficient solutions

## Scope and Constraints

- Max 3h Standard game playtime
- 48"x72" board size
- Normalized game results for tournament play
- About 10 units and 40-50 models per side total
- Set number of 6 game rounds
- Only dice used is D6
- Distances measured in inches, base sizes in mm
- Model agnostic
- Model scale 10mm, i.e. 1 to 200
- Battlefield distance scale is 1 to 1000, each inch roughly equates to 25m

## Non-goals

- Not a detailed simulation with focus on realism
- Not a narrative campaign system
- Not scalable to huge battles
